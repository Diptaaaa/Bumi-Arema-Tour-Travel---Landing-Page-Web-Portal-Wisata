Our Heritage & Passion

Meet Mario & Adrian

Founded in the heart of Chicago by brothers Mario and Adrian, Little Lemon began with a cherished collection of heirloom recipes handed down across three generations of Mediterranean culinary heritage.

Mario focuses on time-honored cooking methods—simmering sauces for hours and hand-rolling fresh pasta daily—while Adrian infuses contemporary culinary flair, sourcing organic citrus, farm-fresh herbs, and cold-pressed olive oils.
